RTL-SDR Quick Access

Browser-based receivers and ham-radio tools

Open receiver

1. Radio Receiver — EA1ITI

Use an RTL-SDR USB dongle directly from a compatible browser. Includes spectrum, waterfall and several demodulation modes.

radio.ea1iti.es ↗ Download / run locally

2. OFFgrid-SDR

Single-file RTL-SDR receiver. Open the project, download its index.html, then open that file in Chrome or Edge and connect your dongle.

github.com/diddy-boy/OFFgrid-SDR ↗ Open SDR page

3. HamDash — SDR Receiver

Open HamDash’s SDR receiver page and explore its SDRcom receiver options.

hamdash.com/sdr ↗
Important: These tools access the RTL-SDR connected to your own device; they are not remote online receivers. Use a Chromium-based browser such as Chrome or Edge. On Android, WebUSB support and USB-OTG compatibility are required. Only one application can use the dongle at a time.
